## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 🏴 Territory Control & Turf Wars

* **Dynamic Zone Influence:** Influence shifts organically based on gang presence, wall tagging, NPC drug distribution, crate captures, and PvP kills against rival members or law enforcement. Reaching 100% influence completely claims the territory.
* **In-Game Zone Builder:** Administrators can create and edit PolyZones, BoxZones, and CircleZones in real time using 3D placement gizmos and live boundary lines.
* **Dynamic Zones Expansion:** Enable map-wide expansion allowing crews to start claiming territories from anywhere on the map simply by spraying their gang tag.
* **Timed Turf Wars:** Gangs can contest rival hoods in timed capture battles featuring customizable zone radiuses, gradual circle shrinkage, capture countdowns, and citywide server alerts.

### 🧪 Underworld Drug Labs & Supply Missions

* **Fully Functional Laboratories:** Includes pre-configured Weed, Cocaine, and Meth labs with custom IPLs, animated worker peds, and a mobile Acid Lab inside the Brickade 6x6.
* **Versatile Ownership Models:** Labs can be claimed through territory dominance, purchased outright with cash/bank/coins, or rented on a recurring daily cycle.
* **Supply Runs & Combat AI:** Refill lab materials through legal purchases or high-risk supply heist missions against hostile NPC guards with 4 difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard, Insane).
* **Automated Processing:** Monitor supplies, manage production times, collect packaged bricks/pouches, and manage upgrades directly through the in-lab laptop terminal.

### 🎨 3D Graffiti Spraying & Corner Drug Dealing

* **Immersive Wall Tagging:** Spray custom text or gang logos on flat walls using spray cans. Features 38+ scaleform fonts, 10+ gang logos (Ballas, Families, Vagos, Cartel, Marabunta, etc.), 14 font colors, daytime/nighttime lighting adaptation, surface material filtering, and paint removers.
* **Street-Level NPC Dealing:** Sell narcotics to local pedestrians using burner phones. Features multiple customer models, 5 customizable tension minigame levels, and auto-selling options.
* **Dynamic Pricing Multipliers:** Payouts scale dynamically based on your gang's territory influence (Low <30%, Mid <70%, High >70%).
* **Realistic Street Hazards:** Buyers can reject low-quality product, attempt to rob the dealer, or trigger silent/audible police and rival gang dispatch alerts.

### 🏢 Gang Management, Rackets & Contraband Airdrops

* **Modern Frosted Glass NUI:** Sleek gang creation, member hierarchy management, and competitive real-time leaderboards (tracking K/D ratios, kills, deaths, influence, and crate captures).
* **Contraband Airdrops:** Call in tactical supply drops using flare sticks. An incoming cargo plane flies over the drop zone, releases a parachuted supply crate, and creates an intense KOS Red Zone.
* **Extortion & Protection Rackets:** Place racket points across controlled neighborhoods that accumulate passive income over configurable intervals, with robbery and collection notifications.
* **Gang Stash & Fleet Garages:** Assign shared gang stashes and fleet vehicle spawn points with configurable total vehicle limits and per-model spawn limits.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Comprehensive Integration:** Integrated with major inventory systems (ox_inventory, qb-inventory, codem-inventory, tgiann-inventory), multiple police dispatches (ps-dispatch, qs-dispatch, cd_dispatch, qb-policejob), and marked bill metadata worth support.
